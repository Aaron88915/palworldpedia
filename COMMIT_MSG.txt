feat(pals): add the 13 Palworld 1.0 Pals we were missing

paldb.cc's live roster lists 299 Pals; our data had 288. The 13 missing are the
1.0 additions - Astralym (the World Tree dragon), Snock Terra and Ribbuny
Botan, the Yakushima bosses and monsters, and the Terraria crossover Pals.

Built from paldb rather than by hand:
  * hover card    -> Paldeck number, element(s), work suitability
  * pal page      -> stats, price, active skills, drops, partner skill,
                     Paldex description
  * /cn/ pal page -> the Chinese equivalents
  * spawner table -> the regions the Pal spawns in
  * paldb CDN     -> the icon, converted to PNG like the other 290 images

Green Slime was already on the site but had taken its attack from the wrong
paldb row (the 1st-level weapon attack instead of the Pal's own attack), with
no movement speed and no price. It is refreshed from paldb as well - it turns
out to be one of the Terraria crossover Pals, which is also why the ten other
Terraria Pals share its "【泰拉瑞亚联动】" description.

Pals that are not in the Paldex (all the Terraria crossover ones) have no
Paldeck number, so the number is hidden on those pages instead of showing
"#000" or "第 0 号帕鲁" - in the header badge, the title, the meta description
and the schema.

Verified: 1816 pages built, 0 errors; 0 broken internal links; 0 empty <a>
tags; every one of the 301 Pals still has both a Chinese and an English
description.

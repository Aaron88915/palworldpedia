feat(ui): show the partner skill on every pal page

pals.json already carried a partnerSkill block for every pal, but nothing in
src/ ever rendered it - it was dead data. The partner skill is one of the most
looked-up Paldex entries, so it now gets its own section between the passive
skills and the drop table.

The Chinese page shows the Chinese name plus the English one in muted text and
the Chinese description; the English page does the mirror image. A pal without
a partner skill (only Green slime) simply has no section, and the description
falls back to the other language when one side is missing.

Also added the partnerSkill field to the Pal type, which it never had, and
the section styling.
